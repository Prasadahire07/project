01 • THE PROBLEM STATEMENT

Why The Current Training System Fails

Vocational and cooperative skilling in India spans thousands of training centres, 14 RICMs, and 19 ICMs. However, without unified digital infrastructure, critical training data remains fractured, vulnerable to proxy fraud, and disconnected from employment.

📁 EXISTING PROBLEM

Fragmented Data Silos

Institutes maintain independent admissions registers, paper roll-calls, exam spreadsheets, and graduation books. If a trainee moves between institutions, their learning record is wiped clean.

No Central Registry Siloed Data
⚠️ CURRENT CHALLENGES

Attendance Vulnerabilities

Traditional sign-in sheets and basic mobile apps are easily cheated via proxy signatures and mock GPS locations. There is zero physical proof that the student was physically inside the classroom.

Proxy Check-Ins No Hardware Proof
📜 LIMITATIONS OF METHODS

Certificates as "Dead Ends"

Once a paper diploma is issued, the connection ends. Paper certificates are easily forged, and employers cannot distinguish between someone who attended every class and someone who barely passed.

Forgery Risks Zero Verification
🚀 WHY NEW SOLUTION

Missing Livelihood & Loop

Training institutes produce graduates with unverified resume claims, while rural cooperatives struggle to find skilled staff. Furthermore, NCCT has no automated outcome tracking to update obsolete syllabi.

No Hiring Pipeline Obsolete Syllabi
✕ EXISTING SYSTEM FAILURES (Status Quo)
✓ OUR PROPOSED UNIFIED SOLUTION
1 Information is scattered across different systems & paper registers
1 One unified platform with a complete, persistent participant master record
2 Attendance is mostly simple check-in (vulnerable to misuse & proxies)
2 Verified presence using smart hardware (ESP32 + BLE + Biometrics)
3 Certificate is treated as the static end point of the journey
3 Certificate leads directly to a verifiable digital Skill Passport
4 Skills are self-declared on resumes without underlying test proofs
4 Skills backed by empirical training, practical marks & attendance metrics

02 • OUR PROPOSED SOLUTION

The 5 Integrated Pillars of Innovation

Rather than isolated point-tools, our platform unifies academic delivery, physical hardware validation, lifelong credentialing, and livelihood placement into a continuous ecosystem.

THE UNIFIED ARCHITECTURAL EQUATION
UNIFIED ECOSYSTEM = LMS + ERP + SMART PRESENCE + SKILL PASSPORT + AI CAREER

One participant holds one complete, permanent digital record from initial registration through lifelong employment in the cooperative sector.

📚 PILLAR 01

Learning Management (LMS)

Academic Core

Modular course delivery with multilingual vernacular support (Hindi, English, regional), voice-guided audio prompts, and offline lesson caching for low-bandwidth rural zones.

  • Vernacular audio & multimedia modules
  • Offline local caching for intermittent connectivity
  • Formative quizzes and practical assessment rubrics
🏢 PILLAR 02

Batch & Academic ERP

Institutional Core

Streamlines candidate nomination, batch scheduling, faculty allocation, classroom logistics, and real-time attendance compliance across all federated institutes.

  • Automated batch allocation & seat quotas
  • Faculty timetable & classroom logistics
  • Multi-institute compliance tracking
⚡ PILLAR 03

Smart Hardware Presence

Hardware (ESP32)

Low-cost ($4-$6) ESP32 microcontrollers paired with Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) classroom proximity beacons and optical biometrics, featuring local flash storage and dynamic QR fallback.

  • Multi-check verification (Biometric + BLE beacon)
  • 100% offline-first local flash buffering
  • Zero-knowledge irreversible minutiae hashing
🛡️ PILLAR 04

Verified Skill Passport

Credentialing

Transforms static diplomas into dynamic proof-of-competence profiles backed by verified test marks, practical evaluations, and attendance telemetry with scannable SHA-256 QR codes.

  • Granular competency levels (Level 1 to 5)
  • Tamper-evident SHA-256 cryptographic seal
  • Instant 2-second QR verification for employers
🎯 PILLAR 05

AI Career & Job Matching

Livelihood Outcome

Intelligent diagnostic engine identifying trainee skill gaps, recommending tailored upskilling pathways, and directly matching verified talent with hiring cooperatives (PACS, Dairy Unions, Banks).

  • AI skill gap diagnostics & course recommendations
  • Direct candidate matching to cooperative job vacancies
  • Longitudinal post-training wage & placement tracking

08 • SECURITY & FRAUD PREVENTION

Robust Security & Anti-Proxy Safeguards

Ground-level vocational classrooms require tamper-resistant engineering. Here is how our hardware, encryption, and role-based access control address real-world vulnerabilities.

🛡️ HARDWARE MULTI-CHECK

Dual-Factor Classroom Presence

Proxy attendance is eliminated by enforcing physical co-presence: the student must authenticate their biometric fingerprint while their mobile device validates the classroom’s Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacon within a 5-meter radius.

✓ Optical Minutiae Template Matching
✓ BLE RSSI Classroom Proximity Validation
✓ Eliminates Geofence Spoofing & Friend Check-ins
🔒 PRIVACY SAFEGUARDS

Zero-Knowledge Biometric Hashes

To respect participant privacy and comply with data protection regulations, raw fingerprint images are NEVER stored on the microcontroller or transmitted across networks.

✓ Irreversible One-Way Mathematical Minutiae
✓ Raw Biometric Images Discarded Immediately
✓ Zero Risk of Biometric Data Leaks
📜 CREDENTIAL INTEGRITY

SHA-256 Tamper-Evident Ledger

Certificates and cumulative skill records are cryptographically sealed with SHA-256 hashes registered in the NCCT central database. Any modification immediately invalidates the QR verification code.

✓ Cryptographically Signed PDF Certificates
✓ Scannable Dynamic QR Code Verification
✓ Public Registry Lookup in Under 2 Seconds
🔄 OFFLINE SECURITY

Encrypted Edge Flash Storage

During rural power or internet outages, offline attendance records stored in the ESP32’s flash memory are encrypted and signed with a unique device nonce to prevent replay attacks upon reconnection.

✓ Encrypted SPIFFS / LittleFS Nonce Logs
✓ Replay-Attack Proof Differential Sync
✓ Conflict-Free Replicated Data Types (CRDTs)
📱 FALLBACK CONTINGENCY

Dynamic Rolling QR Fallback

If the fingerprint sensor smudges or suffers hardware failure, the trainer’s device displays a dynamic time-expiring rolling QR code (changes every 15 seconds) for students to scan, preventing screenshot sharing.

✓ 15-Second Time-Expiring QR Algorithm
✓ Screenshot Sharing Completely Blocked
✓ Authenticated Trainer Manual Override Log
👥 GOVERNANCE & RBAC

Strict Role-Based Access Control

Role separation ensures that trainees cannot alter scores, trainers can only grade their designated cohort, and institute directors have visibility strictly bounded to their campus jurisdiction.

✓ 5-Tier Granular Permission Model
✓ Immutable Audit Logs for Grade Updates
✓ National Ministry Read-Only Oversight Radar
